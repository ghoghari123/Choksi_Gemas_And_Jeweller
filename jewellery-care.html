01
EVERYDAY HABITS

Make Jewellery Care Part of Your Routine

Small everyday habits can help reduce exposure to dirt, oils, cosmetics and accidental impacts.

  • Put jewellery on after applying cosmetics and fragrances.
  • Remove jewellery before activities where it could be exposed to impact.
  • Handle jewellery with clean hands whenever possible.
  • Check your jewellery occasionally for visible changes or loose parts.
Care Tip

If your jewellery suddenly feels different, looks damaged or has a loose stone, stop wearing it and have it inspected.

02
CLEANING

Keep Your Diamonds Looking Their Best

Jewellery can collect oils and everyday residue over time. A gentle cleaning routine can help maintain its appearance.

01
Prepare

Use a clean, suitable work surface away from drains.

02
Clean Gently

Use a jewellery-safe cleaning approach appropriate for the specific piece.

03
Rinse Carefully

If rinsing is appropriate, avoid areas where the jewellery could be lost.

04
Dry Completely

Gently dry the jewellery with a clean, soft cloth.

PROTECT YOUR JEWELLERY

Things to Avoid

Some everyday activities can expose jewellery to unnecessary risk.

Harsh Chemicals

Avoid exposing jewellery to chemicals or cleaning products that may be unsuitable for the materials.

Heavy Activities

Remove jewellery before activities where it could be hit, bent or caught.

Swimming

Consider removing jewellery before swimming or entering environments where it may be lost or exposed.

Bathing & Showering

Removing jewellery can help prevent buildup and reduce the chance of accidental loss.

Cosmetics

Apply lotions, perfumes and cosmetics before putting jewellery on.

Extreme Conditions

Protect jewellery from conditions that may affect delicate materials or settings.

03
STORAGE

Give Every Piece Its Own Space

Proper storage helps reduce scratches, tangling and accidental damage when jewellery is not being worn.

Use a Jewellery Box

Store jewellery in a clean, dedicated jewellery box.

Separate Pieces

Keep pieces separated to reduce contact and scratching.

Prevent Tangling

Keep chains secured and stored individually.

Choose a Suitable Environment

Store jewellery in a clean, dry and stable environment.

PIECE-SPECIFIC CARE

Care for Every Type of Jewellery

01

Rings

Pay particular attention to the setting and prongs, as rings are frequently exposed to everyday contact.

  • Check the setting regularly.
  • Remove before heavy activities.
  • Clean according to the materials.
02

Earrings

Keep earrings clean and make sure their backs and settings remain secure.

  • Check earring backs.
  • Store pairs together.
  • Inspect settings periodically.
03

Necklaces

Keep chains secured and store them carefully to reduce tangling.

  • Fasten the clasp before storage.
  • Keep chains separate.
  • Check clasps periodically.
04

Bracelets

Because bracelets move frequently, inspect their links, clasps and settings regularly.

  • Check clasp security.
  • Inspect links.
  • Avoid high-impact activities.
05

Pendants

Pay attention to the bail, chain connection and stone setting.

  • Check the bail.
  • Secure the chain.
  • Store separately.
06

Bridal Jewellery

Special occasion jewellery deserves careful storage and periodic inspection, especially when worn regularly.

  • Keep pieces together.
  • Inspect settings.
  • Store securely after use.

CARE QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I clean my diamond jewellery?

Cleaning frequency depends on how often the jewellery is worn and the materials involved. Regular gentle cleaning can help remove everyday buildup.

Can I wear my diamond ring every day?

Many diamond rings are designed for regular wear, but removing jewellery during activities that could cause impact, damage or loss is a sensible precaution.

Should I sleep with my jewellery on?

Removing jewellery before sleeping can reduce unnecessary contact and help protect delicate settings and chains.

How should I store diamond jewellery?

Store jewellery in a clean, dry, dedicated jewellery box and keep individual pieces separated to reduce scratching and tangling.

What should I do if my diamond feels loose?

Stop wearing the jewellery and have it inspected by a qualified jewellery professional as soon as practical.

Can I clean every jewellery piece the same way?

No. Cleaning methods should take into account the gemstone, metal, setting and construction of the individual piece.
