Final Checklist
01
START WITH WHAT MATTERS

Set a Budget That Feels Right

Your budget should reflect what you are comfortable investing in the jewellery. There is no universal rule for how much you should spend.

Once your budget is established, you can decide which combination of diamond characteristics gives you the appearance you want.

Helpful Tip

Start with your overall jewellery budget rather than focusing only on the diamond.

  • Consider the complete jewellery piece.
  • Leave room for your preferred setting and metal.
  • Compare diamonds within your comfortable range.

A KEY CONSIDERATION

Don't Overlook Cut

Cut influences how effectively a diamond returns light. When comparing diamonds, consider the overall visual performance rather than focusing on carat weight alone.

WHEN YOU SHOP

Questions Worth Asking

Whether you are shopping online or speaking with a jewellery professional, these questions can help you understand what you are considering.

01

What are the 4Cs?

Ask for the diamond's Cut, Color, Clarity and Carat information.

02

Is it certified?

Ask whether a grading report is available and what it contains.

03

What is the shape?

Make sure the shape matches your preferred jewellery style.

04

What setting is used?

Understand how the diamond will be held and presented.

05

What care is required?

Ask about cleaning, inspection and long-term jewellery care.

06

What documentation is included?

Understand what reports and documents accompany your purchase.
