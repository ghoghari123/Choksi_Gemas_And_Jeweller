DESIGN MATTERS

Shape Is Only the Beginning

The final appearance of a diamond also depends on its cut quality, proportions, setting and the jewellery design surrounding it.

  • Choose a setting that complements the shape.
  • Consider how the proportions look on the finger or neckline.
  • Think about your personal style and everyday wear.
  • Consider the complete jewellery design rather than the stone alone.

SHAPE QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between diamond shape and diamond cut?

Shape describes the outline or form of the diamond, while cut describes how the diamond's facets, proportions and finish affect its interaction with light.

Which diamond shape is the most popular?

Round brilliant diamonds are widely recognized for their classic appearance and strong focus on light performance.

Which diamond shape looks largest?

Visual size depends on dimensions, proportions and shape. Some elongated shapes can create a larger-looking appearance when viewed from above.

Which diamond shape is best for an engagement ring?

The best shape is a personal choice. Round, Oval, Cushion, Emerald, Pear and many other shapes can work beautifully depending on your preferred style.

Can every diamond shape be used in every jewellery design?

Many shapes can be used across different jewellery styles, but the proportions and setting should be chosen to complement the individual shape.
