WHAT IS A DIAMOND?

Nature's Most Remarkable Creation

A diamond is a crystalline form of carbon formed under extraordinary conditions deep within the Earth. Its unique crystal structure gives it exceptional hardness and the ability to interact with light in extraordinary ways.

Every diamond is different. Its proportions, color, clarity and weight combine to create an individual character that makes each stone unique.

A JOURNEY MILLIONS OF YEARS IN THE MAKING

How Diamonds Are Formed

Natural diamonds form under extraordinary conditions deep beneath the Earth's surface and can take immense amounts of time to reach the surface.

01

Deep Within Earth

Carbon exists deep within the Earth's mantle under extremely high pressure and temperature.

02

Extreme Conditions

Intense pressure and heat allow carbon atoms to arrange themselves into the distinctive diamond crystal structure.

03

Journey to the Surface

Volcanic activity transports diamond-bearing rocks toward the Earth's surface.

04

Discovery

Once discovered, rough diamonds are carefully evaluated before being cut and polished.

UNDERSTANDING YOUR OPTIONS

Natural vs Lab-Grown Diamonds

Both natural and laboratory-grown diamonds are made of crystallized carbon. Understanding their origins can help you make an informed choice.

OPTION 01

Natural Diamonds

Natural diamonds formed through geological processes deep within the Earth over very long periods of time.

  • Naturally formed
  • Geological origin
  • Each stone is unique
  • Available with certification
OPTION 02

Lab-Grown Diamonds

Laboratory-grown diamonds are created using advanced technology that reproduces the conditions required for diamond formation.

  • Created in a laboratory
  • Crystallized carbon
  • Similar physical properties
  • Available with certification
KNOW YOUR DIAMOND

Understanding Diamond Anatomy

A polished diamond is made up of several carefully proportioned areas, each playing an important role in how the stone interacts with light.

01

Table

The largest facet on the top of a polished diamond.

02

Crown

The upper section between the table and girdle.

03

Girdle

The outer edge separating the crown from the pavilion.

04

Pavilion

The lower portion of the diamond extending toward the culet.

05

Culet

The small facet or point at the bottom of the diamond.

DIAMOND QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

View All FAQs
What are the 4Cs of a diamond?

The 4Cs are Cut, Color, Clarity and Carat. Together they describe key characteristics of a diamond.

What is the difference between diamond shape and diamond cut?

Shape refers to the outline or form of a diamond, while cut refers to how well a diamond's facets interact with light.

Why is diamond certification important?

A grading report provides an independent assessment of important characteristics of a diamond.

How should I care for my diamond jewellery?

Regular gentle cleaning, safe storage and periodic professional inspection can help maintain your jewellery.
