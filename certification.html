KNOW WHAT YOU ARE BUYING

A Diamond Report Creates Clarity

A diamond grading report documents important characteristics of a diamond according to the grading laboratory's evaluation.

Learning how to read the report helps you compare diamonds using consistent information rather than relying only on appearance or descriptions.

Learn How to Read It
THE BASICS

What Is a Diamond Grading Report?

A grading report is an assessment prepared by a gemological laboratory describing characteristics observed and measured during the grading process.

Professional Evaluation

The diamond is examined using standardized gemological methods.

Written Documentation

Important characteristics are recorded in a formal grading document.

Easier Comparison

A report provides information that can help buyers compare diamonds.

Detailed Information

Reports can contain grading, measurements and identifying details.

READ THE REPORT

Understanding a Diamond Grading Report

Report formats vary by laboratory, but many contain several important categories of information.

01

Report Identification

The report normally includes an identifying number or other reference information.

02

Diamond Characteristics

Key information such as shape, measurements and grading details may be listed.

03

4Cs Information

Depending on the report, Cut, Color, Clarity and Carat information may be included.

04

Proportions & Measurements

Measurements and proportion-related information can help describe the diamond's physical characteristics.

05

Additional Features

Some reports include diagrams, plotting information or additional observations.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Grading Report vs Appraisal

GRADING REPORT

Describes Characteristics

A grading report provides information about a diamond's observed and measured characteristics according to the issuing laboratory's grading process.

  • Describes diamond characteristics
  • Documents grading information
  • Helps with comparison
VS
APPRAISAL

May Estimate Value

An appraisal is a separate service that may provide an estimated value for purposes such as insurance or other financial considerations.

  • May estimate monetary value
  • Can serve different purposes
  • Is distinct from grading
GRADING LABORATORIES

Why the Issuing Laboratory Matters

Different laboratories may have their own grading standards, terminology and report formats.

When comparing diamonds, identify the laboratory that issued the report and review the report details carefully.

Review the complete report

IDENTIFICATION

Laser Inscription

Some diamonds may have a microscopic inscription on the girdle that corresponds to an identification number associated with a grading report.

When applicable, the inscription can provide another way to help connect the physical diamond with its report.

Identification reference
Microscopic detail
Connects physical stone and documentation
