DIAMOND EDUCATION

Diamond Questions

What are the 4Cs of a diamond?

The 4Cs are Cut, Color, Clarity and Carat Weight. They provide a widely used framework for describing important characteristics of a diamond.

What is the difference between diamond shape and cut?

Shape refers to the outline of a diamond, such as Round, Oval, Emerald or Pear. Cut refers to how the diamond has been proportioned, faceted and finished, where applicable, and how those factors influence light performance.

Which diamond shape is most popular?

Round brilliant diamonds are widely recognized for their classic appearance. However, the best shape depends on individual taste and jewellery design.

Does a larger carat always mean a better diamond?

No. Carat measures weight, not overall quality. Cut, color, clarity, proportions, shape and other characteristics also influence a diamond's appearance.

What makes a diamond sparkle?

A diamond's light performance is influenced by its cut quality, proportions, faceting and other characteristics, as well as viewing conditions.

What is diamond clarity?

Clarity refers to characteristics observed within a diamond and, in some cases, on its surface. A clarity grade reflects the grading laboratory's assessment according to its standards.

DIAMOND BUYING

Buying Questions

How should I start choosing a diamond?

Start by understanding your budget, preferred jewellery style and diamond shape. Then compare the 4Cs and review the available grading documentation.

What services does Choksi Gems & Jewellers provide?

Choksi Gems & Jewellers works across diamond sourcing, trading, jewellery manufacturing and finished jewellery. Our team combines product knowledge with considered craftsmanship, clear certification guidance and personal consultation for retail and business requirements.

How much should I spend on a diamond?

There is no universal amount that everyone should spend. Choose a budget that is comfortable for you and consider the complete jewellery design rather than focusing only on the diamond.

Should I choose carat or clarity first?

This depends on your priorities. Consider the overall appearance you want and compare how changes in carat, clarity, color and cut affect the complete diamond.

Should I buy the diamond first or choose the setting first?

Either approach can work. The diamond and setting should ultimately be considered together so that the shape, proportions and design complement one another.

What should I compare when choosing between two diamonds?

Compare the grading information, shape, proportions, appearance, measurements, certification and overall design. Consider which characteristics matter most to you.

Can I get help choosing a diamond?

Yes. You can contact our team to discuss your preferred style, budget and jewellery requirements.

Jewellery Questions

What types of diamond jewellery are available?

Diamond jewellery can include rings, earrings, necklaces, pendants, bracelets and bridal jewellery.

What is a solitaire ring?

A solitaire design typically features a single central gemstone as the main visual focus of the ring.

What is a halo setting?

A halo setting surrounds a central gemstone with smaller stones, creating a framed appearance.

Which metal should I choose?

White gold, yellow gold, rose gold and platinum can create different visual effects. The right choice depends on your preferred style, design and material requirements.

DOCUMENTATION

Certification Questions

What is a diamond grading report?

A diamond grading report is a document issued by a gemological laboratory that describes characteristics observed and measured during its grading process.

Is a diamond grading report the same as an appraisal?

No. A grading report describes characteristics, while an appraisal may provide an estimated monetary value for a particular purpose.

What information can appear on a diamond report?

Depending on the report and laboratory, it can include shape, measurements, carat weight, color, clarity, cut-related information and additional observations.

How can I verify a diamond report?

Identify the report number and use the issuing laboratory's official verification service when available. Compare the verified details with the physical diamond and purchase documents.

Should I keep my diamond report?

Yes. Keep the report and related purchase documentation in a safe place for your records.

JEWELLERY CARE

Care Questions

How often should diamond jewellery be cleaned?

Cleaning frequency depends on how frequently the jewellery is worn and its materials. Regular gentle cleaning can help remove everyday buildup.

Should I remove my jewellery before exercising?

Removing jewellery during activities where it could experience impact, snagging or unnecessary exposure can help reduce the risk of damage.

How should I store diamond jewellery?

Store pieces in a clean, dry jewellery box and keep individual items separated to reduce scratching and tangling.

What should I do if my diamond feels loose?

Stop wearing the jewellery and have it inspected by a qualified jewellery professional as soon as practical.

Can every jewellery piece be cleaned in the same way?

No. The appropriate cleaning method depends on the gemstone, metal, setting and construction of the piece.

Should jewellery be professionally inspected?

Periodic professional inspection can help identify loose stones, worn settings, damaged clasps and other issues that may not be immediately visible.
